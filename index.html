San Juan Academy

The objective of this simulation is to develop the student's ability to recognize and respond to natural wind shifts while sailing upwind. The student will learn to identify headers and lifts by observing the wind indicator, the boat's heading, and the behavior of the sail.

Instructions:

  1. Sail from the starting position toward the Windward Mark.
  2. The simulation will provide a steady 15-knot wind with natural variations in wind direction and one or two gusts.
  3. Watch the ILCA WIND VANE and observe how changes in wind direction affect your boat.
  4. Use the sail and the boat's response to recognize headers and lifts. Use Sheet In, Sheet Out, and Tack when appropriate.
  5. Your goal is to sail efficiently toward the Windward Mark, making good decisions as the wind shifts.
